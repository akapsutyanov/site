Превращение
в героя

by @alexkapustyanov

Четыре промпта, которые превращают тебя в персонажа: LEGO, low-poly, Minecraft и Pixar. Главный принцип — мир остаётся настоящим, меняешься только ты. Лес, свет, боке и глубина кадра сохраняются как на съёмке, поэтому персонаж выглядит вживлённым в реальность, а не наклеенным.

Как пользоваться
  1. Сними или возьми обычное фото/кадр с собой — лучше в полный рост, на природе или улице.
  2. Прикрепи его в Nano Banana / Banana Pro и вставь промпт нужного стиля.
  3. Получаешь стилизованный кадр, где ты — персонаж, а фон остался настоящим.
💡 Хочешь видео — этот кадр можно оживить в Seedance: прикрепи его как стартовый кадр, а исходное видео как референс движения. Ключевая фраза против «расстилизации»: never drift back toward real human.

Стиль 1

LEGO-минифигура

Ты становишься глянцевой пластиковой минифигуркой: цилиндрическая голова, клешни-руки, блочный корпус. Лес и свет остаются настоящими — меняешься только ты.

📎 Блокбастер-эффект, заходит лучше всего
Промпт 
Take the source photo and transform ONLY the man into a LEGO minifigure version of himself. Keep the entire real forest environment completely unchanged — the birch trees, green grass, dirt path, overcast daylight, background bokeh and depth all stay exactly as photographed, real and untouched. Do not stylize the world, only the person.

The man becomes a photoreal LEGO minifigure: glossy injection-molded ABS plastic body, cylindrical head with a printed face, the classic C-shaped claw hands, blocky torso and articulated shoulders and hips. Preserve his identity in minifig form — a molded shoulder-length curly brown hair piece, a printed beard and warm friendly expression, no glasses, wearing a printed grey graphic tee under a dark plaid minifig overcoat. He holds the camera at arm's length with one claw hand at frame-left, keeping the exact same arm's-length selfie composition and pose as the source.

Real plastic material response: subtle specular highlights sliding across the glossy ABS, fine seam lines and stud logic on the parts, a soft-edged contact shadow where the figure sits in the scene, and the forest's green ambient light bouncing naturally onto the plastic so the minifigure sits believably in the real environment — matched daylight direction, matched softness, matched depth of field and haze, no cut-out edges or halos. Photoreal LEGO minifigure composited into a real, unaltered forest photograph. Only the man is LEGO; everything else is real.

Low-poly

Тело собрано из крупных плоских треугольных граней с острыми рёбрами. Геометрия вместо гладких форм — стильно и узнаваемо.

📎 Гранёный 3D · чёткий силуэт
Промпт 
Take the source photo and transform ONLY the man into a low-poly 3D character version of himself. Keep the entire real forest environment completely unchanged — the birch trees, green grass, dirt path, overcast daylight, background bokeh and depth all stay exactly as photographed, real and untouched. Do not stylize the world, only the person.

The man becomes a stylized low-poly 3D character: his whole body built from a small number of large flat triangular facets with sharp visible edges and hard corners, faceted geometric planes instead of smooth curves, simplified angular forms for the head, torso, arms and hands. Flat-shaded polygons with clear boundaries between facets, minimal texture, clean geometric color blocks. Preserve his identity in faceted form — shoulder-length curly brown hair simplified into angular low-poly clumps, a geometric faceted beard, no glasses, wearing a grey graphic tee and a dark plaid overcoat modeled as flat angular polygon planes with simple crease facets. He holds the camera at arm's length with one hand at frame-left, keeping the exact same selfie composition and pose as the source.

Render the low-poly character as a real 3D object sitting in the real scene: flat per-facet shading with crisp edge highlights, a soft-edged contact shadow grounding him on the path, and the forest's green ambient daylight falling across the polygon faces from the matched direction and softness. Match the plate's depth of field, haze and grain so he sits believably in the world — no cut-out edges or halos. A faceted low-poly 3D character composited into a real, unaltered forest photograph. Only the man is low-poly; everything else is real.

Промпт 
Take the source photo and transform ONLY the man into a Minecraft-style voxel character version of himself. His face and clothing are rendered as low-resolution pixel textures mapped onto the cube faces, in the classic 8-bit-per-face Minecraft look. Preserve his identity in voxel form — brown pixel-texture hair blocked around the square head, a simple pixel-texture beard on the face, a friendly pixel expression, no glasses, wearing a grey pixel-texture graphic tee under a dark plaid pixel-texture overcoat. One cuboid arm is raised holding the camera at arm's length at frame-left, keeping the exact same selfie composition and pose as the source.
